Talent Identification & Training Needs (15 minutes)

Core Content

1. Talent Identification & Development

Recognizing high-potential employees (HiPos) and cultivating leadership pathways:

  • Performance vs. Potential Matrix: Distinguishing current task mastery from future leadership capability.
  • Succession Planning: Preparing internal staff to assume critical roles seamlessly.
  • Continuous Engagement: Keeping top talent motivated through progressive growth tracks.

2. Assessing Training Needs

Diagnosing the exact performance gaps that require structured instruction:

  • Performance Discrepancy: Comparing actual employee output against expected standards.
  • Skill vs. Motivation Gaps: Determining whether an error stems from lack of training or lack of incentives.
  • Marketing Context: Identifying gaps in digital analytics, copywriting, or CRM adaptation.

Advanced Lexicon: High-potential (HiPo), succession pipeline, performance discrepancy, competency mapping, experiential acquisition.

• Prompt students to consider how marketing teams spot emerging talent during collaborative campaign projects.

Onboarding, Coaching, Mentoring & Marketing Plan (30 minutes)

Applied Practice

Comparing Training Modalities

Onboarding: Integrating new hires into company culture, workflows, and tools.
Coaching: Short-term, performance-driven guidance focused on specific skill refinement.
Mentoring: Long-term developmental relationship focused on career trajectory and wisdom.
Workshops & OJT: Interactive group seminars and hands-on practical execution.

Team Activity: Marketing Training Plan

In teams, structure a 3-step training plan for junior marketing associates:

  • Step 1 (Week 1 - Onboarding): CRM tool orientation & brand guideline immersion.
  • Step 2 (Week 2 - Coaching): Weekly feedback sessions on social media copy creation.
  • Step 3 (Week 3 - Workshop): Collaborative workshop on data-driven campaign analytics.
Montano Habit: Synergize (Co-creating training structures builds collective team capability).
